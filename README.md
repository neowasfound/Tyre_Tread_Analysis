Tyre Analysis

This was one of our first in-depth data analytics projects. The project investigates whether a spray-on tyre coating can reduce tread wear over distance travelled.

The study involved 30 randomly selected participants, each contributing data on four tyres. Participants were divided into two groups:
Treatment group, 15 participants with coated tyres
Control group, 15 participants with uncoated tyres

We explored the relationship between tyre treatment and tread wear while accounting for additional factors such as distance travelled and the number of shocks experienced.

The analysis was conducted using Python, applying statistical and data analysis techniques to investigate whether the coating has a measurable effect on tyre endurance.
